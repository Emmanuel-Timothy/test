CAMS UGM-COLOR MULTI-PAGE HTML PREVIEW

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. Keep all files together. No React, Tailwind, npm, server, or build step is required.

Palette follows UGM’s published primary colors: UGM Blue (PANTONE P117-16C), UGM Yellow (PANTONE P7-8C), and black. This preview uses #0B457F for UGM Blue and #FFD42B for UGM Yellow.

The preview ships with local SVG artwork so it renders without internet access. The images are illustrative placeholders and should be replaced with approved, locally stored institutional images before production.

Descriptions and article entries are design copy, not verified institutional claims. People and publications pages link to the official directories rather than inventing records.
